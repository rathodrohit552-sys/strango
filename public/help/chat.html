Chat basics.

When you open Strango, the service places you in a waiting queue and matches you with another available user. Send a message when the status says you are connected.

Using Next

Tap Next to leave the current conversation and wait for a new stranger. If the other person disconnects, you can also tap Next to start again.

Typing Status

Typing indicators show only during an active matched chat.
