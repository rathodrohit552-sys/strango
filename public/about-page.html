About

Anonymous chat with a calmer standard.

Strango is a real-time stranger chat platform designed around speed, privacy, and a more trustworthy first impression. The product is intentionally simple in Phase 1: users can start a random anonymous text chat without profiles, feeds, or public identity.

Our Approach

We believe online chat should feel easy to enter and respectful once you arrive. Strango focuses on clean design, clear safety expectations, and simple privacy language so users know what kind of space they are entering.

What Comes Later

Future phases may add communities and interest-based experiences, but this foundation is focused on core chat, support pages, SEO readiness, and trust.
