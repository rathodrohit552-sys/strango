Privacy Policy

Privacy-first expectations for anonymous chat.

Strango is designed to let users chat anonymously. This policy explains the information we may process to operate the platform and keep it reliable.

Information We Process

We may process basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP-derived connection data, and real-time socket connection status. Chat messages are transmitted between matched users to provide the service.

Cookies and Local Storage

Strango may use essential cookies or local storage to remember basic preferences such as cookie consent and to support site reliability.

Safety

Users should not share passwords, financial details, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information in anonymous chats.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@strango.chat.
