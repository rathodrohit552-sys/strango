Guidelines

Respect makes anonymous chat usable.

  • Be respectful and avoid harassment, hate, threats, or bullying.
  • Do not share or request private information such as passwords, addresses, or financial details.
  • Do not post illegal, exploitative, or harmful content.
  • End any chat that feels uncomfortable or unsafe.
  • Use Strango for genuine conversations, not spam or manipulation.
