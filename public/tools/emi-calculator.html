How this EMI calculator works

EMI Calculator FAQ

What is EMI?

EMI means equated monthly instalment. It is the fixed monthly amount paid to repay a loan over time.

Does a higher tenure reduce EMI?

Usually yes, but it can increase the total interest paid across the full loan period.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. Strango's EMI calculator is free and works instantly in the browser.
