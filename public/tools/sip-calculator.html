How this SIP calculator works

A SIP calculator compounds each monthly investment at the expected monthly rate until the end of the selected period. It is useful for estimating long-term mutual fund investment outcomes.

Actual mutual fund returns can vary because markets move up and down. Use this estimate for planning, not as a guaranteed return.

SIP Calculator FAQ

What is SIP?

SIP means systematic investment plan. It lets you invest a fixed amount regularly, often monthly.

What is maturity value?

Maturity value is the estimated final amount, including your invested amount and expected returns.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIP returns depend on market performance and can be higher or lower than the estimate.
