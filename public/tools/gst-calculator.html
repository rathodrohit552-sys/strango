How this GST calculator works

In exclusive mode, GST is added to the entered amount. In inclusive mode, the entered amount already includes GST, so the calculator separates the actual value and tax amount.

This helps businesses, freelancers, and shoppers quickly understand tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing.

GST Calculator FAQ

What is GST exclusive?

GST exclusive means tax is added on top of the entered base price.

What is GST inclusive?

GST inclusive means the entered price already contains GST, so the tax is extracted from the total.

Which GST slabs are included?

This calculator includes common slabs such as 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 40%.
